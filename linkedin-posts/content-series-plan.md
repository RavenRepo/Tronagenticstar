## 🎨 **Content Guidelines**

### **Tone & Voice:**
- **Authoritative but approachable:** Technical depth without intimidation
- **Problem-focused:** Start with real pain points
- **Solution-oriented:** Always provide actionable insights
- **Honest:** Admit trade-offs and challenges

### **Format Consistency:**
- Hook → Problem → Evidence → Solution → Differentiation → CTA
- Use bullet points and white space for readability
- Include relevant hashtags (5-7 per post)
- Add emojis sparingly for visual breaks

### **Technical Balance:**
- Include enough technical detail for credibility
- Explain complex concepts in business terms
- Provide concrete examples over abstract benefits
- Reference specific technologies and patterns
