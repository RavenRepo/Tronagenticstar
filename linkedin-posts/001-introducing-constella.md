I spent 2 years watching AI tools promise everything and deliver frustration.

Cursor AI forgets what I just told it.
GitHub Copilot generates code that breaks our standards.
Windsurf loses context after a few exchanges.

Sound familiar?

Here's the problem: Current AI tools are glorified autocomplete assistants. They don't understand your business, your architecture, or your policies.

So we built something different.

**Introducing Constella** – the world's first Enterprise AI Operating Platform.

Instead of one generic AI assistant, Constella deploys specialized AI agents:

→ **Python Gold-Standard Agent**: Knows your coding standards, internal libraries, and architectural patterns
→ **SecuriShield Security Agent**: Continuously scans against YOUR security policies
→ **QualityQuasar Agent**: Enforces YOUR quality gates and testing standards
→ **Main Architect Agent**: Orchestrates everything like an AI project manager

The game-changer? **Persistent, shared memory.**

Our agents remember everything:
- Your architectural decisions (stored in Neo4j knowledge graphs)
- Your past projects and patterns (semantic search via Qdrant)
- Your specific policies and standards (enforced automatically)

Result: AI that doesn't just assist – it **operates** your entire SDLC with enterprise-grade governance.

And yes, it's 100% self-hosted. Your code never leaves your infrastructure.

We're not building another AI copilot.
We're building an AI engineering organization that works for you.
